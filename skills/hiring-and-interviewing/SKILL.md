# Hiring & Interviewing

Hiring is the highest-leverage, most-regretted decision founders make. Structure beats gut: define what "great" means before you meet anyone, test for it consistently, and decide on evidence.

## When to Activate
- Defining a role / writing a job description
- Designing an interview loop or a specific interview
- Making a hire / no-hire decision
- Closing a candidate

## Before you interview
- **Write the scorecard first.** List the 4–6 outcomes this person must deliver in 12 months and the competencies each requires. This is your rubric — not "seems smart."
- **Job description sells + filters.** Lead with the mission and impact, be honest about stage/scope, list must-haves vs nice-to-haves (short must-have lists widen your pool).

## Run structured interviews
- **Same questions, same rubric, every candidate** — structured interviews predict performance far better than free-form chats.
- **Probe past behavior, not hypotheticals:** "Tell me about a time you…"; then dig: what was *your* part, what did you decide, what happened, what would you change?
- **Assign each interviewer a competency** so you get depth and coverage, not five people asking the same thing.
- **Work samples > whiteboard puzzles.** A realistic task (paid if substantial) is the strongest signal.
- **Take notes tied to the rubric**, during, not after.

## Decide on signal
- **Score independently, then debrief.** Each interviewer submits a rating + evidence *before* the group talks — avoids anchoring/groupthink.
- **Hire for the role's bar, not relative to the day's candidates.** "Would I be thrilled to have them?" If it's a "maybe," it's a no.
- **Reference-check for real** — back-channel, ask specifics, listen for hesitation.
- **Watch for bias** — "culture fit" often means "like me." Assess values and collaboration, not sameness.

## Close
- Sell the mission, the growth, the team; be transparent about comp and stage. Move fast — great candidates have options; a slow, silent process loses them.

## Checklist
- [ ] Scorecard (outcomes + competencies) written before interviews
- [ ] Structured, consistent questions; behavior-based
- [ ] Each interviewer owns a competency; work sample used
- [ ] Independent scores with evidence, then debrief
- [ ] References checked; decision on the bar, not the day's pool
- [ ] Fast, transparent close
