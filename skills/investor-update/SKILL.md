# Investor Update

A consistent, honest investor update is the cheapest way to build trust and unlock help (intros, hires, advice). Investors fund founders who communicate clearly in good times *and* bad. Send it monthly, on schedule, forever.

## When to Activate
- Writing a monthly/quarterly investor or board update
- After a big milestone or a rough month

## The structure (short, skimmable)
1. **TL;DR** — 2–3 lines: the state of the business this month.
2. **Key metrics** — the same core numbers every month (revenue/MRR, growth %, burn, runway, active users, and your north-star). Show the trend, not just the point.
3. **Wins** — what went well (shipped, closed, hired).
4. **Lowlights / challenges** — what went wrong and what you're doing about it. **Do not hide bad news** — this is where trust is built or lost.
5. **Asks** — specific, actionable help: intros to named people/companies, roles you're hiring, expertise you need. Vague asks get nothing.
6. **Runway / financials** — cash, burn, months of runway.

## Principles
- **Consistency > polish.** Same format, same day, every month — even the bad months (*especially* the bad months).
- **Be honest and specific.** Numbers with context. Investors have seen everything; spin erodes trust.
- **Make asks concrete.** "Intro to VP Eng candidates" → nothing. "Anyone who knows [Name] at [Company]?" → results.
- **Lead with the number that matters** (usually revenue or your north-star).
- **Short.** One screen if possible; detail in an appendix/links.

## Checklist
- [ ] TL;DR up top
- [ ] Consistent core metrics with trend
- [ ] Wins **and** honest lowlights + mitigations
- [ ] Specific, named asks
- [ ] Cash / burn / runway stated
- [ ] Sent on a reliable schedule
